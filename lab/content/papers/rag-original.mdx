## What problem does this paper solve?

Knowledge access and provenance. A model that has memorised a fact cannot cite it, cannot be
corrected without retraining, and cannot be audited.

## What mathematical objects are introduced?

The latent-variable factorisation
$$
p(y\mid x) = \sum_{z\in\mathrm{top}\text{-}k}p_\eta(z\mid x)\,p_\theta(y\mid x,z),
$$
in two variants — **RAG-Sequence**, marginalising once per output sequence, and **RAG-Token**,
marginalising at each token. These are
<TopicRef id="rag/retrieval-as-latent-variable-model">different models</TopicRef>, not two
implementations of one.

## What assumptions are made?

That top-$k$ truncation of the sum is adequate, and that the document encoder can be held fixed while
the query encoder is trained (so the index need not be rebuilt during training). The second is an
engineering compromise with a real cost: the document representations are never adapted to the task.

## What equations matter?

The two marginalisations, and the gradient through $p_\eta(z\mid x)$, which is what makes the
training end-to-end: the query encoder receives a signal telling it to up-weight documents that
improved the generation likelihood — an empirical approximation to the
<TopicRef id="rag/retrieval-as-latent-variable-model">true posterior</TopicRef> $p(z\mid x,y)$ that
the variational bound identifies as the right target.

## What theorem or result is important?

No theorem. State-of-the-art on open-domain question answering at the time, with the additional
property that the retrieved passages provide attribution and that swapping the index changes the
model's knowledge without retraining.

## What proof techniques are used?

None.

## What prior mathematics is required?

Marginalisation and latent-variable models; the difference between a mixture of products and a product
of mixtures; dense retrieval geometry.

## Limitations

Only $k\approx5$–$10$ documents fit in context, so the marginalisation is a severe truncation; the
document encoder is frozen; the retrieval distribution over a truncated support is not the true
posterior; and the whole system inherits the
<TopicRef id="rag/retrieval-as-latent-variable-model">recall ceiling</TopicRef> — the generator cannot
use a document the retriever did not return.

## Why it mattered

It is the origin of the term and of the architecture, and it framed retrieval augmentation
probabilistically rather than as a prompt-engineering trick. Almost every subsequent RAG variant is an
intervention on one of the two factors in its central equation.
