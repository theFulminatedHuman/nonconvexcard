## What problem does this paper solve?

Multi-task serving and fine-tuning cost. A $175$B-parameter model fine-tuned for $100$ tasks is
$17.5$T parameters of storage under full fine-tuning.

## What mathematical objects are introduced?

The parameterisation
$$
W = W_0 + \frac{\alpha}{r}BA,\qquad B\in\R^{d\times r},\ A\in\R^{r\times k},\ r\ll\min(d,k),
$$
with $W_0$ frozen, $A$ Gaussian-initialised and $B$ zero-initialised.

## What assumptions are made?

That the fine-tuning update is approximately low rank. The paper offers an intrinsic-dimension
argument and measurements of the effective rank of full fine-tuning updates as evidence; it is not
proved.

## What equations matter?

The parameter count $r(d+k)$ against $dk$, and the fact that $B=0$ at initialisation makes
$W = W_0$ exactly, so training begins from the pretrained function. The merge $W\leftarrow W_0+\frac\alpha rBA$
is what makes inference cost identical to the base model — unlike adapter layers, which add depth.

## What theorem or result is important?

No theorem. Empirically, $r\in\{1,\dots,64\}$ matches or exceeds full fine-tuning on GLUE and on
GPT-3 tasks, with $10^{-4}$ of the trainable parameters in the extreme case.

## What proof techniques are used?

None; ablations over rank, over which matrices to adapt ($W_Q,W_V$ best), and over $\alpha$.

## What prior mathematics is required?

Rank and the SVD; the
<TopicRef id="deep-learning/transformers">low-rank structure of attention heads</TopicRef>, which is
the best available explanation for why low rank suffices.

## Limitations

There is no theory establishing that the optimal update is low rank, and tasks requiring substantial
new factual knowledge show a gap against full fine-tuning. The choice of $r$ and of which matrices to
adapt is empirical. Quantised variants (QLoRA) change the numerics in ways that interact with the
rank choice.

## Why it mattered

It made fine-tuning large models accessible outside well-resourced labs, and it is the default method
for task adaptation. It is also a clean example of a mathematically trivial reparameterisation with
very large practical consequences — the whole content is $r(d+k) \ll dk$.
